Microsoft Intune Increases macOS Agent Check-In Frequency for Faster App and Script Delivery

Saved for offline reading · Open original ↗

Key Takeaways

  • Microsoft Intune Increases macOS Agent Check-In Frequency for Faster App and Script Delivery
  • Newly enrolled macOS devices check in every 3 minutes for the first 15 minutes.
  • After the initial 15 minutes, check-ins occur every 15 minutes for the next 2 hours.
  • Regular Intune management agent check-ins increase from approximately 8 hours to 1 hour.
  • Newly assigned apps, scripts, and policies reach macOS devices sooner.
  • Check-in frequency is controlled by the Intune service, with no admin or user action needed.

Microsoft Intune increases the management agent check-in frequency for newly enrolled macOS devices. During onboarding, devices check in every 3 minutes for the first 15 minutes, followed by every 15 minutes for the next 2 hours. This helps deliver apps, scripts, and assignments faster.
