Your guide to Intune at Microsoft Ignite 2026

Saved for offline reading · Open original ↗

If you manage and secure endpoints, you are already on the front line of your organization’s AI transformation. Trusted, cloud native endpoint management and protection across Windows, macOS, and mobile devices are essential to preparing for AI, securing data and AI, and using AI to strengthen defense. This guide makes it easy to find the Microsoft Ignite 2026 sessions featuring the latest Microsoft Intune management and security innovations, no AI required.

Use this guide to find Ignite sessions and labs, meet the teams behind each, and start thinking about the organizational questions you want to bring to the conference. Bookmark this post at aka.ms/IntuneAtIgnite so you can find it again.

Start with these three sessions for the clearest view of what’s new in Intune and what's coming next.

Tuesday, November 17th, 2:00pm-2:45pm PT

Don’t miss the Intune strategy session for the week. See how Intune turns insights across your digital estate into policy and action—and verifies that each change keeps devices healthy and secure. Learn how IT can gain fleet-wide visibility and control, reduce exposure, and use AI-powered tools to scale its impact.

Speakers: Jason Roszak, VP, Product Management, Microsoft Intune and Eugenie Burrage, Director, Product Marketing, Microsoft Intune

Wednesday, November 18th, 10:15am-11:00am PT

IT’s endpoint estate continues to expand, and deploying a new device is only the beginning. Once deployed, the device must stay configured, patched, and recoverable while apps, policies, and threats change. This session follows a device lifecycle: provisioning, security baselines, app governance, updates, safe deployment of change, and fast recovery when something breaks. See how IT can prevent drift and verify that every change did what it was meant to do in a cloud native environment.

Speaker: Sangeetha Visweswaran, VP of Engineering, Microsoft Intune

Thursday, November 19th, 2:45pm-3:30pm PT

Three questions shape every remediation plan: Where are we exposed? What matters most? How do we fix it without disrupting the business? See how Microsoft helps IT move from finding to fixing beyond the SOC by using AI-powered threat and business context to assess misconfigurations and prioritize risk. Intune and Windows then apply the response, with approvals and staged deployment, and verification keep changes governed and confirm that risk decreased.

Speakers: Alemeshet Alemu, Partner Director of Engineering, Microsoft Intune and Ankur Goyal, Principal Product Manager, Microsoft Intune

Get hands-on with the latest Intune innovations as product experts guide you through a cloud-native endpoint management journey—from provisioning and app delivery to modern policy, updates, and recovery. Bring a demo tenant if you have one, get practical guidance, and take new skills and insights home to fortify your organization’s environment and drive better outcomes.

Experts: Rob York, David Guyer, and Heena Macwan, Principal Product Managers, Microsoft Intune; Jessica Yang, Senior Product Manager, Microsoft Intune; and Ahmed Ghaddah, Product Manager, Windows.

The featured sessions set the direction. The following session and two hands-on labs go further into the work behind them.

Wednesday, November 18th, 3:00pm-3:45pm PT

As agents move into production, IT needs to know which ones are running, what each can reach, and who approves a change to what it does. See how Windows provides the environment agents run in, while Intune, Microsoft Entra, Microsoft Defender, and Microsoft Purview discover, secure, and audit those agents at scale.

Speakers: Daniel Ballard, Principal Product Manager, Windows, Jessica Yang, Senior Product Manager, Microsoft Intune

Tuesday, November 17th - Friday, November 20th (check session schedule for available times)

Configure endpoint security policies, target managed and Defender-connected devices, and check device posture. Then investigate an endpoint security issue, use Defender insights to prioritize the risk, and remediate the affected devices through Intune.

Speakers: Swapna Medi, Principal Software Engineering Manager, Microsoft Intune, Lorena Mora, Senior Product Manager, Microsoft Security

Tuesday, November 17th - Thursday, November 19th (check session schedule for available times)

Assess your organization's security posture and apply Zero Trust principles across identities, devices, threats, and data. Work through strengthening access, reducing exposure, protecting sensitive information, and responding as conditions change.

Speakers: Jon Callahan, Senior Product Manager, Microsoft Security, Lorena Mora, Senior Product Manager, Microsoft Security

Endpoint management delivers value across IT and security teams. These related sessions show how Intune connects with Windows and Microsoft Security to strengthen outcomes across the digital estate.

AI agents run on devices IT manages, use apps IT deploys, and act with access IT governs. That puts the endpoint at the center of moving AI from pilot to practice and connects this year’s Intune sessions: preparing the digital estate ready, governing agents that run on it, and turning security findings into verified change.
