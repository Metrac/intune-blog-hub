Microsoft Intune Adds 10 New Protected Apps for iOS and Android

Saved for offline reading · Open original ↗

Key Takeaways

  • 10 new protected apps are now available for Microsoft Intune.
  • The newly added apps support iOS and Android platforms.
  • Everbridge 360, ShareCRM, and Zoho Mail – Intune support both iOS and Android.
  • Rogo AI is available in two versions: standard and Intune Wrapped for iOS.
  • IT administrators can use these protected apps to support secure app management and data protection through Intune App Protection Policies.

Microsoft Intune Adds 10 New Protected Apps for iOS and Android! Microsoft Intune Protected Apps is a list of mobile applications that support Microsoft Intune’s security and data protection features. These are apps that support Microsoft Intune security policies, helping organizations protect corporate data on iOS and Android devices.

Microsoft Intune Adds 10 New Protected Apps for iOS and Android

Microsoft Intune has added 10 new protected apps to its supported app list. The newly added apps support iOS and Android devices, with some apps available on both platforms and others limited to iOS or Android. These additions expand the range of apps that organisations can use with Microsoft Intune to support secure app management and data protection through App Protection Policies.
