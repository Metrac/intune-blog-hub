Hard Deletion Protection in Microsoft Entra ID to Prevent Accidental and Malicious Deletions

Saved for offline reading · Open original ↗

Key Takeaways

  • Hard Deletion Protection in Microsoft Entra ID to Prevent Accidental and Malicious Deletions
  • Protects critical objects from malicious or accidental hard deletions.
  • Protected objects cannot be permanently deleted for 7 days after soft deletion.
  • Provides time to investigate deletion activities and recover important objects.
  • Once enabled, you cannot remove or disable protection.
  • The protection setting cannot be undone for objects that are protected.

Hard Deletion Protection helps organisations protect critical objects from permanent deletion caused by malicious activities or accidental administrative actions. When enabled, this protection prevents any soft-deleted protected object from being permanently deleted for 7 days.

Prevent Accidental and Malicious Deletions with Hard Deletion Protection in Microsoft Entra ID

This 7-day protection period gives administrators sufficient time to investigate unexpected deletion activities, verify whether the deletion was intentional, and recover important objects if required. It adds an extra layer of security by reducing the risk of losing critical objects permanently.

  • One important consideration is that Hard Deletion Protection is permanent once enabled.
  • Administrators cannot disable, remove, or undo this protection for objects that are already protected.
  • This means organisations should carefully review their requirements before enabling the feature.
  • By enabling Hard Deletion Protection, administrators can improve data protection, reduce the impact of accidental or malicious deletions, and provide additional time to respond to unexpected deletion incidents.
