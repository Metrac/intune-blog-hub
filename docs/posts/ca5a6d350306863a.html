Services

Key services offered by the team of consultants under HTMD Global brand are given below:

  • Standard services
  • Value-added services
  • Strategic transformation services

The standard services are best for the organizations who are looking for a kick start of the project from a modern Intune device management perspective. For example, greenfield tenant setup, basic enrollment, essential app delivery, core policy baseline creation, standard patching solutions, baseline operational documentation, etc. etc.

Value-added services are designed for organizations already running a production environment that want to optimize operations. The Intune consultant team can come in and enhance your deliverables. For example, improve your efficiency, fine-tune RBAC and governance model, security hardening. This helps to keep your Intune healthy and to get good rating in internal and external audits.

The third kind of engagement would be strategic transformation. This is basically looking beyond the BAU and into the future. Helping organizations with topics such as how to enable business to reach the next level by improving your end user computing (EUC) services or workplace services. Defining future-proof posture for endpoint solutions. Also, looking ahead to improve your digital employee experience (DEX).
